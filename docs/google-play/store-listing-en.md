## Full description

Keeply helps you know what you have at home, what is about to expire, and what you need to buy. Organize pantry, cleaning, personal care, medicine, pet and other household products in one clear experience.

YOUR INVENTORY IN ONE PLACE

• Save a product's category, brand, form, unit and location.  
• Track current quantity and minimum stock.  
• Add an expiration date, barcode, notes and a photo from camera or gallery.  
• Search, filter and sort products quickly.

USEFUL LOCAL ALERTS

Review expired, expiring, out-of-stock and low-stock products. Configure local reminders with the cadence and time slots that work for you.

PRACTICAL SHOPPING LISTS

Create a blank list or automatically generate one from products that are out of stock, low or expired. Check items off while shopping and avoid duplicates.

AT-A-GLANCE INSIGHTS

See recent products, upcoming expirations and stock activity. Monthly insights summarize movements and frequently used products with local rules and no generative AI.

FASTER CAPTURE

Take a photo or choose one from your gallery, then move and zoom it inside the frame to choose the crop before saving. Keeply will try to recognize visible label details from the final image on device, such as its name, brand, expiration or code. You can also scan compatible barcodes.

LOCAL-FIRST, NO ACCOUNT

Your profile and inventory stay on your device. Keeply requires no account, contains no ads and does not sell personal data. Image and barcode content is processed on device. ML Kit components may send Google technical metrics and device or app information for diagnostics and service improvement, as explained in the privacy notice.

Keeply is a household organization tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or advice.
