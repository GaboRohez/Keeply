# Prompts y decisiones de identidad visual

## Ícono

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a polished, memorable app icon for Keeply, a local-first home
> inventory app. Show a simple white cube assembled from three rounded geometric panels on a
> vivid indigo #4F6CF1 field, with soft depth and a clean premium mobile-app finish. Centered,
> highly legible at small sizes, no text, no letters, no badge, no border, no Android robot.

Decisiones: cubo como metáfora de inventario organizado, geometría compatible con el launcher
adaptativo, blanco e índigo alineados al color principal de la interfaz.

## Gráfico destacado

> Use case: ads-marketing. Create an exact wide 1024x500 Google Play feature graphic for Keeply.
> Use Image 1's white three-panel cube as the brand mark. Place it on a vivid indigo #4F6CF1
> environment with tidy shelves, household containers, pantry products and clean inventory cards
> with subtle status checks. Friendly polished 3D illustration, generous central safe area,
> cohesive with the app icon, no text, no slogan, no Google Play badge, no device frame.

Decisiones: sin texto para reutilización regional, elementos del hogar para explicar el propósito
y foco central para tolerar los recortes promocionales de Google Play.

## Ilustración superior para las capturas promocionales

> Use case: ads-marketing
> Asset type: reusable decorative header illustration for Google Play screenshot cards
> Primary request: Create a compact, polished 3D illustration cluster representing a local-first home inventory app.
> Scene/backdrop: transparent background.
> Subject: a small indigo storage cube, a pantry jar, a cleaning bottle, a tiny shopping basket, and a checklist card with a simple check mark, grouped tightly as one balanced emblem.
> Style/medium: friendly premium soft 3D illustration, rounded geometry, subtle shadows, clean app-store marketing style.
> Composition/framing: centered compact horizontal cluster, generous transparent padding, readable at about 180 pixels tall.
> Lighting/mood: soft studio light, calm and organized.
> Color palette: Keeply indigo #4F6CF1, white, pale lavender, mint, warm apricot accents.
> Constraints: no words, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no device frame, genuinely transparent background, crisp clean edges.
> Avoid: busy scene, photorealism, watermark, gradients extending into the transparent padding.

Decisiones: la ilustración se generó sin texto y luego se compuso de forma determinista con las
capturas reales. Así se conserva íntegramente la interfaz y los títulos en español no dependen de
texto generado por IA.
